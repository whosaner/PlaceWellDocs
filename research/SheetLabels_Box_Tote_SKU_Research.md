Research date: 2026-10-24

Status: Research only. No implementation changes were made.

Official-template typography/QR POC:

`Docs\research\poc\box-tote-labels\README.md`

## Executive conclusion

PlaceWell's current `SL125-XW` stock is a 1.5-inch round, 30-up US Letter
sheet made from SheetLabels' laser-only White Polyester Weatherproof material
(`XW`). SheetLabels offers the same XW face stock across many larger templates.

The strongest initial candidates for boxes and totes are:

1. **SL522-XW - 4 x 5 inch rounded rectangle, 4 per sheet**
2. **SL573-XW - 4 x 3 inch rounded rectangle, 6 per sheet**
3. **SL1437-XW - 2.5 x 2.5 inch square, 12 per sheet**
4. **SL128-XW - 3.5 inch round, 6 per sheet**
5. **SL517-XW - 6.5 x 4.5 inch rounded rectangle, 2 per sheet**

Recommended research path:

- Treat **SL522-XW** as the primary large-tote/large-box candidate.
- Treat **SL573-XW** as the primary medium-tote/box-end candidate.
- Treat **SL1437-XW** as the compact high-yield candidate.
- Keep **SL517-XW** for high-visibility large storage applications.
- Keep **SL128-XW** only if preserving the round design is strategically
  important.

The material is weatherproof, tear-resistant polyester, but this does **not**
prove that its adhesive will hold reliably on every plastic tote or corrugated
box. Adhesion testing is mandatory before selecting a production SKU.

## 2. XW material facts

Official XW size/material pages:

- https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/all-label-sizes/white-poly-laser-weatherproof-laser-only
- https://www.sheetlabels.com/materials/sheet/white-poly-laser-weatherproof-laser-only/spec

Verified material properties:

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Short code | `XW` |
| Face stock | 2.5 mil top-coated matte white laser PET |
| Material type | Polyester |
| Finish | Matte |
| Adhesive | BP-52 Permanent |
| Liner | 3.5M LF liner |
| Printer compatibility | Laser |
| Service temperature | -40 F to 200 F |
| Minimum application temperature | 32 F |
| Published uses | Outdoor labeling, equipment/assets, containers, HDPE drums, barcodes |
| Compliance statements | CONEG, REACH, RoHS, CPSIA, BS5609, FDA 21 CFR 175.105, latex-free |

Important interpretation:

- The **face stock** controls print/media durability.
- The **adhesive** controls whether the label remains attached to the target
  surface.
- "Weatherproof polyester" does not by itself prove adhesion to polypropylene,
  polyethylene, textured plastic, dusty cardboard, or damp surfaces.

SheetLabels describes polyester as comparatively rigid and less conformable
than vinyl on curved or uneven surfaces. It also states that its sheet labels
are not laminated, so printed output may weather faster than a laminated roll
label.

## 4. Ranked shortlist

### 1. SL522-XW - recommended large format

Official page:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/SL522-XW

Official PDF template:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/mm/78776066680ce221c225d35f7cc4cf01

Why it fits:

- 4 x 5 inches gives ample room for a large label name and the existing
  0.5-inch QR.
- Large enough to identify a tote from a useful viewing distance.
- Rounded corners are preferable to square corners on rigid polyester.
- Four-up capacity is simple and predictable.

Risks:

- The downloaded official vector template centers the columns at approximately
  0.156-inch side margins with an approximately 0.188-inch horizontal gap.
  These vector coordinates supersede the earlier manually transcribed
  0.125-inch margin.
- Artwork must remain safely inset from the sheet edge.
- Higher per-label material use than medium formats.

### 2. SL573-XW - recommended medium format

Official page:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/SL573-XW

Official PDF template:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/mm/3d922939b229e77b3f0b0c658500cd7d

Why it fits:

- 4 x 3 inches remains highly readable.
- Six labels per sheet improves yield.
- Suitable for tote ends, shelves, and medium box faces.
- Rounded corners reduce lifting risk relative to square corners.

Risks:

- No vertical gap; registration must be measured carefully.
- Narrow side margins still require controlled printer alignment.

### 3. SL1437-XW - recommended compact/high-yield format

Official page:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/SL1437-XW

Official PDF template:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/mm/6d000434d15f8e62516c4d816f05d821

Why it fits:

- 2.5 x 2.5 inches is materially larger than current labels without dominating
  a smaller tote.
- Twelve per sheet gives the best yield among the shortlist.
- Supports a larger name and the current QR.

Risks:

- Square corners can lift sooner on rigid polyester.
- Less visibility than the 4-inch formats.

### 4. SL128-XW - round alternative

Official page:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/SL128-XW

Official PDF template:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/mm/9ab4d41bea055c718963a2611659beec

Why it fits:

- Preserves PlaceWell's current round design language.
- No square corner to lift.
- Much greater visibility than the current 1.5-inch round label.

Risks:

- Only six labels per sheet.
- Very small vertical gap (0.05 inch) demands precise registration.
- A large round label may use tote/box space less efficiently than a rectangle.

### 5. SL517-XW - extra-large alternative

Official page:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/labels/SL517-XW

Official PDF template:

https://www.sheetlabels.com/mm/535442a8b28df93921bce0ebc4884634

Why it fits:

- Maximum visibility for large warehouse/storage totes or moving boxes.
- Ample room for name, QR, category, and optional secondary information.

Risks:

- Only two labels per sheet.
- High unit material consumption.
- Excessive for ordinary household totes.

## 6. Surface-specific risks

### Smooth polypropylene/polyethylene totes

- PP/PE are low-surface-energy plastics.
- XW lists container and HDPE drum labeling as common uses, but SheetLabels
  does not publish tote-specific peel/adhesion values.
- Treat suitability as unverified until tested on the exact tote resin and
  finish.

### Textured/ribbed/curved tote walls

- Rigid polyester is less conformable than vinyl.
- Rounded corners are preferable.
- Apply only to the largest flat, smooth panel available.
- Avoid seams, ribs, molded lettering, and compound curves.

### Corrugated cardboard

- SheetLabels does not publish a corrugated-board guarantee.
- Dust, recycled fibers, coatings, and surface roughness can reduce adhesion.
- Permanent adhesive may tear fibers or leave residue during removal.

### Cold or damp surfaces

- XW has a 32 F minimum application temperature.
- The service range does not mean it can be applied successfully to a wet,
  condensing, or contaminated surface.
- Test application at realistic temperature and humidity.

### Reusable totes

- Permanent XW may be difficult to remove and may leave residue.
- RXW is an available removable-material option on matching templates.
- Removable adhesive may be less secure on low-energy plastic; test both.

## 7. Required physical validation

Before choosing a production SKU:

1. Request XW material samples from:
   https://www.sheetlabels.com/samples
2. Buy or obtain the exact shortlisted templates when possible.
3. Test on representative:
   - Smooth PP tote
   - Textured PP/PE tote
   - New corrugated box
   - Recycled corrugated box
4. Clean half the plastic samples and leave half in normal-use condition.
5. Record adhesion at:
   - Initial application
   - 24 hours
   - 72 hours
   - 7 days
6. Check:
   - Edge lifting
   - Bubbling
   - Sliding
   - Residue/removal
   - Flexing and handling
   - Damp/cold behavior
7. Print at Actual Size and verify:
   - Sheet feed
   - Registration at every position
   - Toner adhesion/scuffing
   - Curl/warping
   - QR scanning
   - Stack handling

## 8. Decision gates

Do not choose the final vendor SKU until the owner decides:

1. Primary target: household tote, moving box, warehouse tote, or mixed.
2. Required viewing distance.
3. Permanent versus removable application.
4. Whether a single SKU must cover both cardboard and plastic.
5. Whether rounded corners are required.
6. Required labels per kit and acceptable sheet waste.
7. Whether the existing 0.5-inch QR remains the production size.
8. Whether additional text/category information belongs on the larger label.
9. Whether in-house variable printing is mandatory.
10. Whether XW adhesion passes the exact target-surface tests.

## 9. Recommendation

Run the first physical comparison with:

- **SL522-XW (4 x 5)** for maximum practical tote visibility.
- **SL573-XW (4 x 3)** for a balanced medium format.
- **SL1437-XW (2.5 x 2.5)** for compact/high-yield use.

Use the existing Xerox C320 controlled print workflow:

- Adobe Reader
- Actual Size / 100 percent
- Color
- Label media
- Main tray

Do not add a PlaceWell SKU to production configuration until one of these
templates passes both printer registration and target-surface adhesion tests.
