DESCRIPTION  (limit 4000)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything in its place, done well.

PlaceWell turns premium QR-coded labels into a calm, practical home organization system. Apply a PlaceWell label to a jar, bin, basket, drawer, or container, scan it with your phone, and create a simple record of what belongs there.

No account is required. PlaceWell is local-first, designed for quick setup and everyday recall at home.

What you can do with PlaceWell:

- Scan a PlaceWell QR label to set it up or open it later
- Name each label with a clean 4-step setup flow
- Add a photo so you can recognize contents at a glance
- Assign a room and zone, such as Pantry, Kitchen, Bathroom, Laundry, or Entryway
- Record contents and notes for bins, jars, baskets, and storage containers
- Search your labels and filter by room or zone
- Revisit recently scanned labels from the home screen
- Import a whole label kit by scanning an Order QR
- Apply a room and zone to a full kit, then adjust individual labels when needed

Made for spice organization:

PlaceWell includes special support for spice labels, so the app remembers what the jar cannot.

- Track Best By dates
- Track In Use Since dates
- See freshness status such as Fresh, Use soon, Past best-by, or Not tracked
- Save brand and notes
- Tap Refilled today to update refill history
- Let PlaceWell learn your refill pace over time

PlaceWell is especially useful for:

- Spice jars and pantry containers
- Dry goods and baking supplies
- Tea, coffee, and snack storage
- Bathroom and laundry organization
- Linen closet baskets
- Entryway bins and household catch-alls
- Giftable home organization kits

PlaceWell by BeNiralu LLC is built for people who love an organized home but do not want cluttered systems, spreadsheets, or another required account. The design is quiet and premium: soft Porcelain Sky colors, simple flows, and just enough structure to make every label useful.

Scan. Set up. Find it again.

PlaceWell is a companion app for physical QR-coded home-organization labels. No account, login, or payment is required.

The core flows (label setup, scan-to-recall, bulk import) begin by scanning a PlaceWell QR label. Reviewers will not have a physical label, so we have ATTACHED a QR sheet (REVIEW-SHEET-all-qr.png) with demo labels.

TO REVIEW ALL FEATURES:
1. Launch PlaceWell. On the Home screen, tap the round Scan button at the bottom center.
2. Point the camera at a QR on the attached sheet:
   - "Smoked Paprika (spice) - SETUP" opens the label setup flow ("Make it Unforgettable"): enter a name, optionally add a photo, pick a room and zone, then Save. The label then appears on the Home carousel.
   - Tap the saved label to open its detail / scan-to-recall view (spice labels show freshness such as Best By and In Use Since).
   - "Order QR - BULK IMPORT" loads a kit of labels you can import at once.

Camera and photo-library permissions are used only to scan QR codes and attach label photos. All label data is stored locally on the device; no account and no sign-in.

 4) APP PRIVACY   (App Privacy -> Get Started)
########################################################################

Q: Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?
A: NO  ->  select "No, we do not collect data from this app".

Why (matches the shipped build, same as Google Data Safety):
   - All label data (names, contents, notes, PHOTOS, room/zone, freshness)
     is stored ONLY on device. Apple counts data as collected only if it
     leaves the device; yours does not.
   - No analytics SDK, no crash SDK, no ad SDK in the build (the Firebase
     Analytics wrapper is inert - package not installed).
   - The only network call (QR lookup) sends just the scanned label/order
     ID + a static token; no personal data, no account, no device ID.

Result: your listing shows "Data Not Collected".
