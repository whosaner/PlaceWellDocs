Everything in its place, done well.

PlaceWell is a premium home-organization app for QR-coded physical labels. Apply a PlaceWell label to a jar, bin, basket, drawer, or container, scan the QR code with your phone, and create a simple record of what is stored there.

PlaceWell is local-first and does not require an account, so setup stays fast and calm.

Organize your home with QR labels:

• Scan a PlaceWell QR label to set it up or open it again later
• Name each label in a guided setup flow
• Add a photo for quick visual recall
• Assign a room and zone, such as Pantry, Kitchen, Bathroom, Laundry, or Entryway
• Save contents and notes for bins, jars, baskets, and containers
• Search your labels and filter by room or zone
• Open recently scanned labels from the home screen
• Import a full kit by scanning an Order QR
• Apply one room and zone to a kit, then adjust labels individually

Built for spice freshness:

When spices move into matching jars, the original packaging often disappears. PlaceWell helps the app remember what the jar cannot.

• Track Best By dates
• Track In Use Since dates
• See freshness status: Fresh, Use soon, Past best-by, or Not tracked
• Save brand and notes
• Tap Refilled today to update refill history
• Let PlaceWell learn your refill pace over time

Great for:

• Spice jars
• Pantry containers
• Dry goods and baking supplies
• Tea, coffee, and snacks
• Bathroom storage
• Laundry supplies
• Linen closet baskets
• Entryway bins
• Household catch-alls

PlaceWell by BeNiralu LLC pairs minimalist QR labels with a companion app that makes everyday organization easier. It is designed for a calm, premium home: simple visuals, clear flows, and no cluttered account setup.

Scan. Set up. Find it again.
