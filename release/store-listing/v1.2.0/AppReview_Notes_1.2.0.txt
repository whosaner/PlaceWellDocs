PlaceWell 1.2.0 — App Review Information

PlaceWell has no account, login, purchases, subscriptions, or shared
user-generated content. Personal inventory data stays on the user's device.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2.0
On first use (no labels yet), the Home screen shows a brief guided hint pointing
to the round Scan button ("Scan your PlaceWell Order Label"). After scanning an
Order QR, a short 3-step walkthrough explains bulk setup. These hints are purely
informational and are dismissed with "Got it" / "Skip".

FUNCTION AND TARGET AUDIENCE
PlaceWell is the companion app for physical QR-coded home-organization labels used
on spice jars, pantry containers, storage bins, drawers, and moving boxes. Scanning
a label opens its digital record, where the user can add a name, photo,
contents/notes, room, zone, and freshness information. It helps home organizers
keep an accurate digital record of what is stored behind each physical label and
recall it later.

SETUP AND REVIEW ACCESS
No credentials or account are required.
Demo QR codes are attached (REVIEW-SHEET). They cover Setup, Recall, Detail, and
Order QR bulk import.

Review steps:
1. Launch PlaceWell. On the empty Home screen, tap "Got it" on the scan hint, then
   tap the round Scan button.
2. Grant camera permission.
3. Display the attached QR sheet on another screen and scan a code.
4. Complete Label Setup: add a name, optional photo, room/zone, and notes; Save.
5. Open the saved label from Home to view, search, edit, or update it.
6. Scan "Order QR - Bulk Import" to import a complete label kit (the guided
   walkthrough appears on this screen).

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- PlaceWell QR Service (Linode) validates issued QR labels and returns non-personal
  label/order metadata; supports bulk import and deep-link/download fallback pages.
- Google Cloud Firestore stores issued-label metadata (label ID, printed name,
  category, SKU, order association, status, aggregate scan counters).
- Apple App Store/TestFlight and Google Play distribute the app.
- Expo/React Native modules provide camera, photo picker, haptics, local audio,
  and local storage.

User-entered contents, notes, room/zone assignments, freshness info, and photos are
stored locally on-device and are NOT stored in Firestore. PlaceWell uses no
authentication provider, payment processor, advertising SDK, AI service, or
cross-app tracking.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. PlaceWell functions consistently across all regions.
