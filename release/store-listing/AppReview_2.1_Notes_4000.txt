3. FUNCTION AND TARGET AUDIENCE

PlaceWell is the companion app for physical QR-coded home-organization labels used on spice jars, pantry containers, storage bins, drawers, and moving boxes. Scanning a label opens its digital record, where the user can add a name, photo, contents/notes, room, zone, and freshness information.

It helps home organizers and label customers maintain an accurate digital record of what is stored behind each physical label and quickly recall it later.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES

- PlaceWell QR Service on Linode validates issued QR labels, returns non-personal label/order metadata, supports bulk import, and provides deep-link/download fallback pages.
- Google Cloud Firestore stores issued-label metadata such as label ID, printed name, category, SKU, order association, status, and aggregate scan counters.
- Apple App Store/TestFlight and Google Play distribute the app.
- Expo/React Native device modules provide camera, photo picker, haptics, local audio, and local storage.

User-entered contents, notes, room/zone assignments, freshness information, and photos are stored locally on-device and are not stored in Firestore. PlaceWell uses no authentication provider, payment processor, advertising SDK, AI service, or cross-app tracking.
