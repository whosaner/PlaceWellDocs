PlaceWell is a companion app for physical QR-coded home-organization labels. No account, login, payment, or subscription is required.

The core setup and bulk-import flows begin by scanning a PlaceWell QR label. Reviewers will not have physical labels, so REVIEW-SHEET-v1.1.0.png is attached.

REVIEW STEPS

1. Launch PlaceWell and tap the round Scan button at the bottom of the Home screen.
2. Tap Continue and grant camera permission.
3. Display the attached QR sheet on another screen.
4. Scan "Label Setup - Basil" to open the guided setup flow. Add an optional photo, select a room and zone, and save the label.
5. Open the saved label from Home to view, search, edit, or update it.
6. Scan "Order QR - Bulk Import" to load a reusable test order containing 15 labels across the round, rectangular, and square SKUs. The order includes spice stock artwork, storage fallbacks, and generic-container fallbacks.

User-entered label names, photos, contents, notes, locations, and freshness data are stored locally on the device.
