## 4. Listing Description

**Beautiful organization you can actually use.**

Meet **PlaceWell by BeNiralu** — premium QR-coded home organization labels designed to help you keep track of what is stored in your jars, bins, baskets, and containers. These are not ordinary pantry labels. Each label connects to the PlaceWell app, so a quick scan can open the label record and show what is inside.

If you love a clean, minimalist home but also want organization that is practical, PlaceWell was made for you.

### What it is
PlaceWell labels are physical, premium home organization labels with a printed QR code. Use them on spice jars, pantry containers, kitchen canisters, bathroom storage, laundry containers, entryway bins, and more.

### How it works
1. Apply your PlaceWell label to a clean, dry surface.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone.
3. Open the PlaceWell app and assign what is stored there.
4. Re-scan any time to quickly see what belongs in that container.

For spice labels, PlaceWell can also support freshness tracking, making it easier to keep tabs on what you have opened and what should be used first.

### Why customers love it
- Premium minimalist look
- Functional QR code for smarter organization
- Works across multiple rooms in the home
- Ideal for pantry, spice, kitchen, bathroom, and storage containers
- Thoughtful gift for organized-home lovers

### What is included
Each pack includes your selected quantity of PlaceWell QR-coded labels, professionally prepared for home organization use.

Available pack options:

- **1.75 x 1.75 in** rounded square — ideal for pantry fronts, canisters, baskets, and larger containers

### Material
Printed on **white weatherproof polyester label stock** for a clean, premium finish that fits modern interiors.

### Best uses
- Spice jars
- Pantry canisters
- Dry goods containers
- Tea and coffee jars
- Bathroom storage
- Laundry containers

### Care instructions
- Apply only to a **clean, dry, smooth surface**
- Press firmly after applying
- Allow adhesive to set for **24 hours** before heavy handling
- Wipe gently with a soft damp cloth if needed
- Do not soak
- Do not place in the dishwasher
- Avoid rough scrubbing or abrasive cleaners

### FAQ
**Q: Are these just decorative labels?**  
A: No — PlaceWell labels are designed to work as part of the PlaceWell organization system. Each label includes a QR code that can be scanned to open the associated label record in the PlaceWell experience.

**Q: Do I need the PlaceWell app?**  
A: Yes — the QR feature is what makes PlaceWell different from standard labels. The app connection is part of the product value.

**Q: Can I use these only for spices?**  
A: Not at all. They work beautifully for pantry items, jars, bins, baskets, bathroom storage, laundry supplies, and general home organization.

**Q: Which shape should I choose?**  
A: Round works best for lids and spice tops, rounded rectangle works well for narrow containers and bottles, and rounded square is ideal for front-facing pantry containers and larger bins.

**Q: Are the labels waterproof?**  
A: They are made from weatherproof polyester stock and are suitable for normal home use with gentle wiping. For longest life, avoid soaking and dishwashers.

**Q: Are containers included?**  
A: No. This listing is for the labels only.

**Q: Is this a good gift?**  
A: Yes — PlaceWell makes a beautiful and practical gift for anyone who loves an organized, elevated home.
