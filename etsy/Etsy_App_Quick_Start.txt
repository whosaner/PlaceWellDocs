HOW TO USE YOUR PLACEWELL SMART LABELS

Your label set includes an Order QR that helps you download the free PlaceWell app and set up all your labels together.

1. DOWNLOAD PLACEWELL

Scan the included Order QR with your phone's Camera app. Tap the link, then download the free PlaceWell app from the App Store or Google Play.

2. LOAD YOUR LABEL SET

Open PlaceWell and tap the gold Scan button. Allow camera access, then scan the Order QR again. Your complete label set will appear in the app.

3. SET UP YOUR LABELS

Review the labels, choose their room and location, then tap Create Labels. You can also open an individual label to add a photo, notes, or other details.

4. STICK, SCAN, AND STAY ORGANIZED

Apply each physical label to its container. Scan its QR anytime with PlaceWell to view or update what is stored there.

Keep the Order QR until your label set has been successfully added to the app.
